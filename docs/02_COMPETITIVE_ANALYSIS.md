# 02. Competitive Analysis

## 1. Market Overview
The subscription tracker market is polarized. On one end are **automated, high-cost services** (Rocket Money) that require bank access. On the other are **manual, free-to-start utilities** (Bobby, Subby) that rely on aggressive paywalls for basic features like adding more than 5 items.

**The Opportunity**: There is a vacuum for a **"Honest Premium Utility"** — a low-cost, one-time purchase app that offers unlimited tracking and privacy without the "bait-and-switch" of a free tier that hits a paywall immediately.

## 2. Top Competitors

### 2.1 Bobby - Track Subscriptions (iOS only)
*   **Status**: Market Leader (Aesthetics).
*   **Model**: Free + In-App Purchase (Pro ~$2.99).
*   **Strengths**: Beautiful, iconic design; great typography; clean UX.
*   **Weaknesses**:
    *   **iOS Exclusive**: No Android version.
    *   **Paywall Trap**: Limits specific features (like iCloud sync and Dark Mode) and number of subs in free tier.
    *   **Reliability**: Recent reviews cite missed notifications.
*   **Gap to Exploit**: "We don't charge you extra for Dark Mode or to add your 6th subscription."

### 2.2 Subby (Android & iOS)
*   **Status**: The Standard Utility.
*   **Model**: Free (limited to 5 subs) + Pro Upgrade (~$9.99).
*   **Strengths**: Functional, simple, privacy-focused (no bank linking).
*   **Weaknesses**:
    *   **Aggressive Limit**: Free version capped at ~5 subscriptions, which is too low for modern users.
    *   **Price**: The $9.99 Pro upgrade is steep for a simple database app.
    *   **UX/UI**: Android Reviews describe it as "functional but clunky" — lacks the polish of Bobby.
*   **Gap to Exploit**: Beat their Pro price by 90% (£0.99 vs $9.99) and offer a far superior UI on Android.

### 2.3 Rocket Money (formerly Truebill)
*   **Status**: The Heavyweight.
*   **Model**: Subscription ($6–12/mo).
*   **Strengths**: Automated (scans bank), negotiates bills, full financial suite.
*   **Weaknesses**:
    *   **Privacy Nightmare**: Requires linking bank accounts (Plaid).
    *   **Cost**: Expensive recurring monthly fee to track other recurring fees.
    *   **Complexity**: Bloated with features users don't need just to track generic bills.
*   **Gap to Exploit**: "The Privacy Alternative." No bank linking, no data harvesting, no monthly fee.

### 3.2 Key Attack Vectors (Marketing Angles)

1.  **"The Anti-Subscription App"**:
    *   *Copy*: "Why pay a subscription to manage your subscriptions? Boby/Rocket want monthly rental. We want 99p once."
2.  **"Privacy First"**:
    *   *Copy*: "Your bank details stay with your bank. We don't want them, and we don't need them."
3.  **"Uncapped Freedom"**:
    *   *Copy*: "Most trackers limit you to 5 items unless you pay $10. SubSentry is unlimited from day one."

## 4. Conclusion
We are not competing on "Automation" (Rocket Money). We are competing on **Value and Design**. By offering the feature set of "Subby Pro" ($9.99) for the price of £0.99, and wrapping it in a design identifying as premium as Bobby, we can capture the segment of users who want a manual, private tracker but refuse to pay $10 or a monthly fee for it.
