## ASO NOTES & RATIONALE

### Keyword Strategy
- **Primary terms targeted**: subscription tracker, bill reminder, renewal alert, subscription manager
- **Long-tail targets**: free trial reminder app, no bank subscription tracker, private bill tracker
- **Differentiation terms seeded**: "no bank linking", "no monthly fee", "unlimited subscriptions" — these double as SEO terms and objection-handling copy
- **Avoided**: "budget planner", "expense tracker" — adjacent but different intent; would attract wrong audience

### Conversion Strategy
The 99p price is both the core objection and the core selling point. Copy is structured to:
1. Validate the pain (subscriptions surprise you)
2. Name the problem with alternatives (bank linking, paywalls, iOS-only)
3. Position SubSentry as the honest alternative
4. Justify the price by contrasting it with competitors' pricing
5. Remove residual risk ("your data never leaves your phone")

### A/B Test Candidates (post-launch)
- **Subtitle A**: `No bank link. No monthly fee.` (objection-led) vs **B**: `Track bills. Kill surprises.` (outcome-led)
- **Short Description A**: Current (privacy-led) vs **B**: Alternative A (honesty-led)
- **Promotional Text**: Rotate seasonally without submitting an update
